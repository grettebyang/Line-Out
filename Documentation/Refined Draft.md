# Refined prototype
A brief overview of all the parts of the game. There are heaps of documents in the google drive for a more detailed overview. Note: When it says explore as prefix, it’s intended for future prototyping/tuning.

## Game overview: 
Casual multiplayer arcade-like game, linear level structure, sandbox mechanics, hints of realism to add to the immersion.

## Game theme and game feel:
Lighthearted, bright colors, limited embellishment, leans more toward realistic but stylized effects.

## Components: 
Individual dog movement: Responsive, realistic, turning radius determined by velocity, explore acceleration, explore friction.

## Ropes:
Rope collision is not precise but a collective of the entire dog configuration system, no stretch, only visibly.

## Sled movement:
Realistic movement, test, car based, rigidbody, code driven, bounce off objects, explore hp vs packages vs no consequences, destroy smaller objects when run into, explore different sled cargo, package types determine sled physics, max and min weight of sled.

## Mechanics: 
Rhythm Mechanics: Fill up gauge and trigger power-ups, explore team based rhythm combos, separate gauges, customizable individual power-ups, both button prompts and howl pitches for right joystick.

## Setting/environment: 

## Map layout:
Linear path, alternative paths, explore minimaps.

## Checkpoints:
Resets negative afflictions, respawn point, 1 checkpoint per minute, change package load at checkpoints, pass through.

## Level select:
Each level is selectable from one menu.

## Survival:
Hunger level, pick up food along the way, slowly decreases, if it gets all the way down you will slow down, could give a slight boost, framing to make it a benefit rather than a drawback.

## NPCs:
wolves attacking dogs or packages to slow you down, birds, AI with basic following mechanics, turrets, obstructing AI, and bark to scare them away.

## Gameplay and dynamics: 

## Level Design:
Static, explore movable obstacles to avoid

## Camera:
Camera hints for certain events, camera placement behind sled, limited camera effects to reduce motion sickness

## Mission:
Mission on top of goal of getting to end of level, different one in each level
